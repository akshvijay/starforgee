Mission BriefingAbout the Event

StarForge is a 24-hour intercollege hackathon hosted at BMSIT&M where student teams from across colleges build solutions inspired by space exploration, from satellites and rovers to mission-control software and life support. Come with an idea, leave with a prototype.

  • 24 hours of building
  • 6 problem statements
  • 2 tracks: Hardware & Software
  • ₹0 registration fee
🛰 6 problem statements will be released: 3 for the Hardware track and 3 for the Software track. Pick one and build.

Choose your craftTwo Tracks

🔧

Hardware Track

Sensors, microcontrollers, robotics, embedded systems and IoT. Build working physical prototypes that could survive a mission.

3 problem statements

💻

Software Track

Web, mobile, AI/ML, data and simulation. Build the software that powers missions, analyses data and keeps crews safe.

3 problem statements

Round 1Online Screening (PPT)

Teams first submit a short presentation online. Shortlisted teams get an invite to the 24-hour on-campus finale.

  1. Register your team on Unstop.
  2. Pick a problem statement once released.
  3. Download the PPT template and fill it in.

    Who can participate?

    Any college student currently enrolled in a recognised institution. This is an intercollege event, so teams from any college are welcome.

    What is the team size?

    Teams of 2 to 4 members. [Confirm]

    Is there a registration fee?

    No. Registration is completely free, and you register through Unstop.

    Can teams include members from different colleges?

    Yes. [Confirm]

    Do we need a ready idea to register?

    No. Problem statements are released later; the screening round needs a PPT on your approach.

    What should we bring?

    Laptops, chargers, college ID, and hardware components for the hardware track. Basic infrastructure and refreshments are provided. [Confirm]
